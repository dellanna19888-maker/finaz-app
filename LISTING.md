## 3. Lange Beschreibung (Capterra / G2 / Website)

**EN:**

## 4. Kern-Features (Bullet-Liste)

- 🧠 **Chat command center** — type what you need, the AI proposes, you approve
- 🎬 **Content plan** — idea → script → recording → edit → published pipeline, by chat
- 📊 **Channel check** — paste your numbers + competitors, get concrete improvements (no API key)
- 💰 **Finances** — income & expenses with balance, categories, and an AI overview
- 📚 **Knowledge base** — your notes in one place; the AI generates & summarizes
- 🛡️ **Compliance gateway** — every AI action is checked & logged (privacy-aware)
- 🔑 **Bring your own key** — Claude/Anthropic or free Google Gemini, no vendor lock-in
- ↩️ **One-click undo** · 📱 **PWA (installable)** · 💾 **JSON export** · 🏠 **self-hostable**
